## 5️⃣ Travel Tips for Mumbai (Budget‑smart & Safe)

| Tip | Details |
|-----|---------|
| **Transport** | • **Local trains** are the cheapest & fastest for longer hops (₹10‑₹30). <br>• Get a **One‑Day Unlimited Train Pass** (₹110) if you’ll move a lot in a day. <br>• Use **auto‑rickshaws** for short trips; always insist on the meter (or agree on ₹30‑₹40 per km). |
| **Stay Connected** | Buy a prepaid **Jio/Paytm** SIM (₹250 with data) at the airport – works everywhere. |
| **Dress Code** | Modest clothing when visiting religious sites (cover shoulders & knees). Comfortable shoes for walking on uneven sidewalks. |
| **Safety** | Keep valuables in a hidden pouch. Night travel: stick to well‑lit areas, prefer trains or app‑based cabs (Ola/Uber). |
| **Money** | Carry a mix of **cash (₹500/₹2000 notes)** and a **debit/credit card**. ATMs are plentiful, but fees may apply. |
| **Weather** | October‑November is pleasant (25‑30 °C). Carry a light rain‑coat or umbrella as occasional showers occur. |
| **Bargaining** | Common in street markets (Crawford, Chor Bazaar). Start at 50 % of the asking price, settle around 70‑80 %. |
| **Health** | Stick to freshly cooked food, avoid raw salads from questionable stalls. Carry **antacids** and **basic first‑aid** (just in case). |
| **Timing** | Most museums close by 5 pm; plan visits early. Trains peak between 8‑10 am & 6‑8 pm – travel off‑peak for a seat. |
| **Apps** | • **M‑Trains** (Western/Eastern line timings) <br>• **Google Maps** for walking routes <br>• **Zomato/Swiggy** for food‑delivery price checks (helps budgeting). |
| **Cultural Etiquette** | • “Namaste” or simple “Hello” is fine. <br>• Respect queuing norms; Mumbai locals are patient but busy. |

### Quick Checklist Before You Go
- ✔️ Print/Save hostel confirmation & airport‑to‑city shuttle ticket.  
- ✔️ Pre‑load INR on a prepaid card or have enough cash.  
- ✔️ Pack light: 1‑2 shirts, 2‑3 t‑shirts, comfortable jeans/shorts, a light sweater, flip‑flops + sneakers, a small umbrella.  
- ✔️ Bring a reusable water bottle, travel-size hand sanitizer, and a basic medication kit.
