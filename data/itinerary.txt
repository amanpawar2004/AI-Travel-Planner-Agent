## 5️⃣ Travel Tips & Hacks

| Tip | How it Helps |
|-----|--------------|
| **Best time to visit** | **Oct‑Feb** – pleasant weather (15‑28 °C). |
| **Local transport** | Download **“Pune M‑Way”** for bus routes, and **“Ola/ Uber”** for quick rides. Auto‑rickshaws have a fixed fare of ₹25 for first 2 km. |
| **Dress code** | Light cottons; carry a **light jacket** for evenings at Sinhagad (can get breezy). |
| **Safety** | Pune is safe, but keep an eye on belongings in crowded markets. |
| **Cash vs. Card** | Small stalls accept only cash; keep **₹1,000‑₹2,000** in small notes. Most restaurants accept cards and UPI. |
| **Wi‑Fi** | Free Wi‑Fi at most cafés, malls, and the **Pune Metro** stations (if you use the metro). |
| **Cultural etiquette** | When visiting temples or the Ashram, dress modestly (no sleeveless tops) and remove shoes. |
| **Stay connected** | Get a **local SIM** (e.g., Jio, Airtel) – ₹200 for 10 GB data, lasts the whole trip. |
| **Emergency numbers** | Police: 100, Ambulance: 108, Tourist Helpline: 1800‑425‑4444. |
| **Packing list** | Comfortable walking shoes, reusable water bottle, sunscreen, power bank, small day‑pack. |
